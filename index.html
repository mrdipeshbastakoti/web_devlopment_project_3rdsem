ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT

A modern computing education with a global perspective.

Our department combines strong computing foundations with hands-on learning and emerging technologies. Students learn how software works, how intelligent systems are built, and how technology can solve real problems.

Originally created for international students in 2024, the program became the Department of Intelligence Computing in 2025.

01

Strong foundations

Programming, algorithms, databases, networks and computer systems provide the technical base.

02

Practical learning

Projects and hands-on assignments help turn classroom concepts into real development skills.

03

Future technologies

Students explore AI, machine learning, cloud computing, data science and related areas.
